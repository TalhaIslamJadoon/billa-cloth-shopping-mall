About The Mall

A Household Name on the Karakoram Highway

Sitting right on the N-35, Karakoram Highway side in Abbottabad, Billa Cloth Shopping Mall has grown into one of the area's largest destinations for cloth and general garments. Families from across Hazara come here to dress for every occasion — daily wear, Eid, weddings and the winter season.

Inside, floor after floor of fabric rolls, stitched garments and seasonal collections means you rarely leave empty-handed — and rarely pay more than you should. It is the kind of place where three generations of the same family have shopped.
